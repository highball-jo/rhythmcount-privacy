RhythmCount: Rep Counter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

RhythmCount ("the app") is developed by JoCoding, Inc. This policy explains what data the app handles and what never leaves your device.

Camera and motion data stay on your device

RhythmCount uses your device's camera to detect body pose landmarks and count repetitions. All camera frames and pose data are processed entirely on-device. The app does not record, store, or transmit any images, video, or pose data. Nothing you do in front of the camera ever leaves your device, and we cannot see it.

Data collected by third-party services

The app shows ads and offers an optional one-time purchase. The following third-party SDKs process limited data for those features:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, with your permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt), IP address, and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy. In regions where required, a consent form (Google UMP) is shown before ads are initialized, and you can decline personalized ads.
  • RevenueCat (purchases) — processes anonymous app-user identifiers and purchase history to deliver and restore the one-time "Unlimited Rounds" purchase. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

What we do not collect

  • No account or sign-up is required; we collect no names, emails, or contacts.
  • No camera images, video, or pose/motion data are collected.
  • No precise location data is collected.
  • Your rep counts and settings are stored only on your device.

Tracking

With your permission (iOS App Tracking Transparency), the advertising SDK may use your device's advertising identifier to deliver personalized ads. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized using that identifier.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new "Last updated" date.
